How the call is made

  1. Download about 1,000 candles each on 1 hour, 4 hour, and daily (Binance public klines, paginated if needed).
  2. On each timeframe: RSI(14) (Wilder), SMA 20 vs SMA 50, volume vs its 20-bar average, and confirmed swing highs/lows (5 bars either side) as nearby support / resistance.
  3. Daily sets the trend (short average above long = up, below = down).
  4. 4 hour is the trigger: RSI under 30, a bullish SMA cross, or a bounce near support (and the mirror for sells).
  5. 1 hour is timing: do not buy into nearby resistance / overbought; do not sell into nearby support / oversold.
  6. All three must line up for BUY or SELL. Anything mixed is HOLD. Volume colours the sentence; it does not override the stack.

Honest numbers, classroom rules. Not a crystal ball, not a broker, not financial advice.
